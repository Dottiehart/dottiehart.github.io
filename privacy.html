Privacy Policy

Dottie Assistant · Last updated October 29, 2026

Dottie Assistant ("the app") is a private, single-user tool. The account owner's assistant, Dottie Hart, uses it to manage the Google account dottiehart.ea@gmail.com. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how that data is used, stored, and shared.

Data the app accesses

  • Gmail: message headers (sender, recipients, subject, date), message content, labels, and drafts in the authorized account.
  • Google Calendar: calendars and events in the authorized account.
  • Basic account information: the email address of the authorized account, used to confirm which account is connected.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the assistant features the account owner asked for: summarizing and organizing email, drafting replies, sending messages after the account owner approves each one, and viewing or changing calendar events after the account owner confirms each change. The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles for any other purpose.

How the data is stored

The app runs on a personal computer owned by the account owner. The Google sign-in token is stored in that computer's system keychain. Email and calendar content is fetched when needed. Short summaries and notes may be kept in the assistant's local files on that computer. Nothing is stored on servers run by the app.

How the data is shared

To read, summarize, and draft messages, the app sends the relevant email or calendar content to Anthropic's Claude API for processing. Anthropic acts as a service provider under its commercial terms. Summaries and drafts are delivered to the account owner through a private Telegram chat. Google user data is not shared with anyone else, except where required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request, the locally stored token and any locally kept notes will be deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to dottiehart.ea@gmail.com.
