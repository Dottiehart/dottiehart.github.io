Dottie Assistant

A private executive-assistant tool for the account owner.

Dottie Assistant is a personal, single-user application. It lets the account owner's assistant, Dottie Hart, work from the Google account dottiehart.ea@gmail.com on the account owner's behalf.

With the account owner's permission, the app uses Google APIs to:

  • read and organize email in that Gmail account, and draft replies;
  • send email, but only after the account owner has reviewed and approved each message;
  • view and manage events on that account's Google Calendar, after the account owner confirms each change.

The app is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. It acts only on the one Google account that authorized it.

Read the Privacy Policy to see how Google user data is handled.
